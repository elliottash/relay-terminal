Ctrl+Enter on an empty prompt box continues a stopped turn (#SXF1)

With the box empty and the agent idle, the send-now key now means "continue"
when the last turn stopped at its step or tool-call limit (m_limitReached,
unchanged) or the pane was restored from a session whose last turn never
ended: the worker reports that as state_loaded {turn_open} (previous commit)
and the pane keeps it in m_turnCutOff, cleared wherever m_limitReached clears
and on every done. The rule lives in relay::continueturn::sendNowContinues,
tested headless like relay::queuesubmit; a non-empty box and a busy agent keep
every meaning they had. Per the standing hint rule the slow paths teach the
fast one: the /continue hint and the ▸ Continue link name agent.interrupt's
first binding (agent.continue stays unbound), the palette row matches, and
both Keymap descriptions now tell the truth about the empty-box path.
