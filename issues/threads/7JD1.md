Claimed by the `enter-resumes` session (Claude Opus 5, subagent). Working the three steps in order: the worker first, then the pane and a card's console, then the remote protocol.

**The wire choice, made in step 1 and landed first** (`2d2993e1`): the **worker** resumes on the submit, not the pane sending `resume_queue` after its `ask`. The card left it open; this is the side that leaves a terminal pane's wire byte-for-byte unchanged *whatever* the queue is doing, not only when nothing is paused — the pane sends nothing new in either case — and it is the same code that makes a device's `ask`/`board_ask` resume, which step 2 asks for anyway. `TurnSupervisor.submit` clears `_paused` on its way past, after the `busy` check that reads it, for everything except `origin: "relay"` (a background subagent reporting back must not undo somebody's Stop).
