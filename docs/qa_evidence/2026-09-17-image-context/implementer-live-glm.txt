Run B — OpenRouter (deepseek/deepseek-v4.1-flash), the refusal
--------------------------------------------------------------
The stored openrouter key is present, so this is the real preset; the point is that **no request is
made at all**.

    preset=openrouter model=deepseek/deepseek-v4.1-flash key=<from keyring, not shown> vision=False
      [vision_unavailable] {"event": "vision_unavailable", "turn_id": "t5c63f3b5-1",
                            "model": "deepseek/deepseek-v4.1-flash", "images": 1,
                            "text": "deepseek/deepseek-v4.1-flash cannot read images and no vision
                             model is set. Choose one under Settings > Models > Vision model, or

What this proves: a preset without vision and with no vision model configured refuses the turn with
a message naming what to do, before anything is sent.
