1. What is the notification for? Today the agent side exists: a finished background subagent's result is handed to the main agent before its next model call, and if the pane is idle an automatic "relay"-origin turn is queued (relay_core/subagents.py, `subagent_handoff`: wake / next_model_call / pending). But the human side does not: `subagent_finished`/`subagent_handoff` reach only the Subagents panel and the transcript (src/SubagentsPanel.cpp:197,219) — no bell row, nothing that pulls you back to the pane. My recommendation: the ask is the human-facing bell notification (a "Subagent finished" row in the window's notification list, click jumps to the pane), riding on the existing handoff event. Or did you mean guest agents' child sessions (Codex/Claude) notifying their parent too?

2. The name — I'd advise against "subrouting": "routing" is already the word for model routing here (the Models pane's Routing tab, cards #00G1/#495G), so "subrouting" will read as "which model a subagent runs on", not "a finished subagent telling you". The mechanism already has a precise internal name — `handoff` (the `subagent_handoff` event) — and the tool copy already says the result is "delivered to you". My recommendation: keep **handoff** for the mechanism and call the bell row a plain **subagent-finished notification**; no new coined word needed. If you want a friendlier product word, "report-back" or "check-in" beat "subrouting".
