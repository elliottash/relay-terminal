Plan refined against the code (see `## Plan`). The four decisions re-read with that in hand, recommendation first; two are sharper than before and one is new.

1. **Surface.** Recommend: the existing Projects tab of Sessions & Projects becomes the portfolio view; no new top-level pane and no Board pane over the global Board yet. Reasoning: #P7SJ deliberately held that pane at three tabs and #EA37 decision 7 declined a project home page. The Projects tab already has the discovery half (registry, pinned, live sessions, open Board). Adding portfolio fields to its rows is the smallest change that gives the record a visible place. A board layout of projects can come later as a global-Board tab reading the same cards.

2. **Record home.** Recommend: a `project` card type in the global Board, with machine roots staying in `state/projects.json` keyed by the card id. Reasoning: card types with their own folder, statuses and fields are a worn path in `board.py`; ids, threads and `board_links` come free. The registry is explicitly machine-specific and removable, which is exactly the portable-versus-local split the research asks for, so nothing portfolio-shaped should go into it.

3. **First release.** Recommend: steps 1–5 as one slice (type, registry link, Projects tab, cross-project work cards, updates and stale filters). Budgets are their own card. Reasoning: a record with no visible place to create and review it repeats the maintenance failure the research documents. Step 5 is small once the thread entry exists.

4. **Budgets.** Changed recommendation: agent spend per project with actuals first, planned hours as a plan-only field, both in a later card. Reasoning: Relay has a real actuals source for model spend (per-session cost plus session-to-workspace-to-project) and none for human hours; an unmetered hours budget is decoration, and pane focus time is not billable time. Question for you: is plan-only hours enough, or do you want an opt-in focus-time meter behind it?

5. **New: work cards in the global Board.** Step 4 lets a work card live in the global Board if it names at least one project. That narrowly reverses #916B ("no board outside a project", the Globals agent's "not a fallback inbox" line). Recommend yes, with the "must name a project" rule enforced by `check`. The alternative is to keep cross-project work in one of the linked projects and mark it there.

Answers to 1, 2 and 5 unblock step 1.
