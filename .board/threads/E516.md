Step 1 done, and the diagnosis is not what the plan guessed. On this machine `codex debug models` (codex-cli 0.155.1) exits 0 in ~60 ms and `catalog_rows` parses it correctly: gpt-6-astra, gpt-5.6-sol, gpt-5.6-terra, gpt-5.6-luna, gpt-5.5 (hidden rows dropped). The real bug: the first `presets` answer must not wait for the scan (by design, tested), so it carries codex `models: []` — and when the scan lands milliseconds later, **nobody re-sends `presets`**. The worker never pushes the catalogue and Options renders from the pane's cached copy without re-asking, so the row stays a text field even though the worker's catalogue is complete. The fallback catalogue alone would not reach the GUI either. Implementing: (a) the worker emits a fresh `presets` event when the scan completes, (b) the pane refreshes open settings panes on that event, (c) the plan's fallback list served only after the scan has completed empty.
