# sweet-street — dossier

## What it is and what we were trying to do

"A calm Edwardian animal-collecting game… You put things out in a garden, cats come, and you learn what each one wants by noticing" (`README.md`). One repository holds three games and an inheritance: the archived Dog Club MVP at the root (tag `mvp-v1`, `DOG-CLUB-DESIGN.md`), Sweet Street v1 in `street/` (Godot 4.7, portrait 720×1280, shipped as a browser game at `sweetstreetlullaby.com/play/`), Sweet Street Gardens in `gardens/` (226 GDScript files, the new direction since 2026-09-07), and the Pondkeepers corpus in `inherited/pondkeepers/`. A Python server is authoritative for the world (`server/streetsim/`), with a WebSocket service, a voice service and a band service beside it. Domain: game design, illustration, synthesised sound and physics research, plus a live web deployment. The Steam registration deadline of 31 August 2026 is "Elliott's alone" (`README.md`).

Size (verified): 621 commits from 2026-08-09 to 2026-09-21, 551 with `Co-Authored-By` (Claude Opus 5 212, Claude Fable 5 111, Claude Fable 5.1 92, plain "Claude" 99). Working tree 67 GB, of which `build/` is 31 GB, `street/` 5.6 GB, `gardens/` 4.2 GB, `art/` 2.1 GB, `archives/` 2.6 GB. `SWEET-STREET-DESIGN.md` is 1,671 lines with tuning tables in pence "so they can be checked". Commit subjects are full sentences ("The shelf can scroll and does not: two measurements that disagree, and neither is wrong").

## How the work was actually done

- **Instruction files**: `WARP.md` (95 lines of dated user instructions: one branch and one checkout on 9 September, a session-start reminder on 14 September, bakes on `spark` on 16 September), `CLAUDE.md` (377 lines: map, gotchas, conventions), `AGENTS.md` (points at both, plus the Relay block), `docs/PLAN_AND_QA_CONTRACT.md`.
- **Board**: a file tracker in `issues/` inherited from reproduction-checker: 216 files, fields `Status`, `Milestone`, `Workstream`, `Component`, `Owner`, `Dependencies`, `Acceptance`; `INDEX.md` generated by `tools/build_issue_index.py`. Counts: open 72, needs-qa 67, in-progress 36, done 29; `needs_qa/` subfolders hold 65 files. `.board/` was initialised 2026-09-19 and has zero cards; `.switchboard -> .board`, and `board.yaml` still says "Switchboard configuration".
- **AI tools and delegation**: overnight goals in `docs/sweet-street-gardens/overnight/<date>/GOAL.md`; Claude CLI Opus delegation with GLM/Kimi fallback authorised 7 September, GLM withdrawn 10 September for Codex subagents (`WARP.md`); Gardens workers require a validated task manifest and releases an acceptance record (`docs/sweet-street-gardens/workflow/READINESS.md`). Parallel agents share one checkout "with explicit, disjoint file ownership"; no landing tool exists here.
- **Scripted vs manual**: CI with six gates (`.github/workflows/ci.yml`, added "after the 2026-08-10 review: a GDScript parse failure reached HEAD because nothing ran the Godot gates automatically"); `tools/release.sh` "the one road to the live site"; 131 entries in `tools/`, 33 named `check_*`, 15 of them `*_browser`. Manual: art picks, listening, the Deck, the audience test.
- **State between sessions**: `docs/sweet-street-gardens/workflow/READINESS.md` and `acceptance.json` (gate ledger A0–A5), `build/release_logs/<stamp>/`, `build_info.json` provenance in the shipped site, and the dated instructions in `WARP.md`.

## Cases and servers

- *Release the site*: `tools/release.sh` runs gates, export with provenance stamp, pack split, gzip sidecars, site assembly, `web_smoke.py` locally, upload, swap, `web_smoke.py` live; `--no-deploy` is the rehearsal (three rehearsal logs from 2026-09-16). A program built after a card-sized failure.
- *Check one surface*: one `check_*` tool per surface (`check_gardens_rooms_browser.py`, `check_gardens_listening.py`, `check_gardens_deck.py`, …), each a small verification server.
- *Keep two simulations honest*: `tools/parity_check.py`, "the one with teeth"; `server/tests/test_covenant.py` makes a design promise a property of the code.
- *Choose an art style*: the person, from images. `art/street/STYLE.md`: "Elliott picked from images, not descriptions"; Treatment A chosen, B confined to in-world print, C retired. Rejected work is archived, not deleted (`prototypes/archive/2026-09-08-animal-bench/`).
- *Track art provenance*: `art/street/library/index.html` and `catalog.json`, `docs/sweet-street-gardens/ART_LIBRARY_AND_PROVENANCE.md`, `gardens/assets/art/provenance.json`.
- *Playtest with strangers*: `docs/audience-playtest.md`, a protocol for ten people with a diagnostic gate of eight unaided openings.
- **Redone ad hoc**: three worktrees diverged and were consolidated on 9 September (`docs/WORKSPACE_CONSOLIDATION_2026-09-09.md`); the frog was drawn as an A0 sheet, a four-animal batch and a rejected pixel bench before the art gate was written; byte-pinned audio suites had to be re-pinned when work moved from the laptop to `spark` (`WARP.md`).

## Verification

`docs/QA.md` is the record: "two faults sat on the live site for days while every gate was green: the game rotated a quarter turn in every landscape browser window, and no data pack ever mounted… Both were found by Elliott, playing." Its lessons are the ones this dossier is for: test the artefact, not the recipe; assert outcomes, not proxies; silence by design needs a back door; and "a person looks at a screenshot".

Modes present: **script** (server pytest, seeded Godot sim, `--rotate-test` as a truth table, content validation); **probe on the shipped artifact** (`web_smoke.py` in a real browser at two viewports, `web_throttle.py` at 300 KB/s); **AI-on-visual only as a machine check** (`qa_tour.py` flags stand-ins and overflow, "then LOOKED AT"; `DEFECTS.md` is what the person writes); **human listen** (`needs_qa/` "audio LISTENED to"); **human play** on a device; **pairwise/choice** for art. Unverified and said so: `docs/visual-qa-2026-09-06.md` "not a claim of real-device or human-playtest approval"; `ANIMAL_ART_CONTRACT.md` "current code-drawn animals have not met the SS1-reference art gate"; every `READINESS.md` entry ends "required human/device review stays open".

## Strengths

- The release road encodes the incident that created it, twice (before upload and on the live URL).
- The `Acceptance` field names the check, and `needs_qa` notes must say "what a machine could not establish".
- Art decisions are made from images, recorded with the images, and rejections are kept.
- `PLAN_AND_QA_CONTRACT.md` refuses "an indefinite 'pending human review' footnote while development proceeds past it".
- Design arithmetic is checkable, and the covenant is a test.

## Weaknesses and limitations of the ad-hoc workflow

- **65 items in `needs_qa/` for one reviewer**, whose checks are looking, listening and playing.
- **Three games and an inheritance in one 67 GB repository**, `build/` alone 31 GB, GitHub name still `dog-club`.
- **Three delegation regimes in one week** and a fourth (the Relay board) installed and unused.
- **Canonical checkout by machine**: `~/apps/_games/sweet-street` on the laptop, `~/repos/sweet-street` on `spark`, "one machine's checkout at a time"; worktrees had already diverged once.
- **Host-dependent pins**: byte-pinned suites and `OMP_NUM_THREADS=1` renders make a test failure ambiguous between code and machine.
- **Art gate unmet while content expands**, which the contract documents were written to stop.
- **Status in bullet fields** with ten distinct spellings in the tree (`open`, `needs-qa`, `closed`, `resolved`, `fixed`, …); notes files (`issues/notes-*.txt`) beside the tracker.
- **`.env` in the tree** (mode 0600, not opened), `.probe_quota` at the root, 20 uncommitted entries at the time of reading including issues moved to `done/`.

## What Relay would have to support here

- **Look, listen, play as verification modes** on the card, with the device and viewport named, so `needs_qa` notes become fields.
- **The release road as a server**: gates, provenance stamp, rehearsal, live smoke, with each run's log attached to the card that shipped.
- **Browser and device probes as panes**: `web_smoke.py` at 450×800 and 1280×800 is the shape; a Deck session is the same shape with a person.
- **Art decision objects**: candidates, the chosen image, the rejected ones kept, and the reference the next generation must anchor to.
- **Worker manifests and acceptance records as cards**, replacing `READINESS.md` prose with a ledger.
- **Machine-scoped policy**: which host renders, which host pins, which checkout is live.
- **One checkout, many agents**: disjoint ownership and serialised engine sessions are stated rules here with no tool behind them; the relay-terminal `land.py` problem, unsolved.
