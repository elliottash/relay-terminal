# Relay trademark policy

**DRAFT — for the owner's review. Not legal advice.**

The name **Relay**, the phrase **Relay Terminal**, the Relay icon, the domain `relay-terminal.ai`
and the application id `org.relayterminal.Relay` (the "marks") identify the terminal published at
`relay-terminal.ai` by Elliott Ash. They are not registered at the time of writing; this policy
says how they may be used, so that "Relay" keeps meaning this program and its official builds.

The **code** is free software under AGPL-3.0-or-later. Nothing here limits what the licence lets
you do with the code. This policy is only about the name and the icon, which the licence does not
cover.

## You may, without asking

- Redistribute unmodified Relay — the source, or a build made from it without changes — under the
  name Relay, including in a distribution's package repository, as long as you say where it came
  from and do not claim it is an official build if it is not.
- Say that your software "works with Relay", "is built on Relay" or "is a fork of Relay", and use
  the name in a truthful description, a review, an article, a talk or a bug report.
- Package Relay for a distribution or a format (deb, Flatpak, AppImage, AUR and the like) under the
  name Relay, with the packaging changes that format needs, as long as the program is otherwise
  unchanged and the package says who built it.
- Use the name in a link to `relay-terminal.ai` or to the repository.

## You may not

- Call a **modified** version "Relay" or "Relay Terminal", or use the Relay icon as its icon, or
  ship it under the application id `org.relayterminal.Relay`. Give your fork its own name, icon
  and application id, and it may say it is based on Relay.
- Suggest that Elliott Ash endorses, supports or is connected with your product, service, fork,
  build or event, unless he has agreed in writing.
- Use "Relay" or a confusingly similar name, or the icon or a close copy, as the name or logo of a
  product, service, company, domain or social-media account, whether or not the product has
  anything to do with terminals.
- Use the marks in a way that suggests an official hosted service. In particular, a gateway or
  rendezvous of your own is not "Relay Free" or "the Relay rendezvous"; those names belong to the
  services the maintainer operates.

## Forks in practice

The AGPL lets you fork; this policy asks you to rename. A fork that changes behaviour should change
the product name in the window title and the About box, the icon, the application id and the
metainfo, and should not point its update or hosted-service settings at `relay-terminal.ai`
unless it is the unmodified program. Keeping the copyright and licence notices is required by the
licence and is welcome.
