Worker tells a resumed pane its turn was left open (#SXF1)

`conv_index.turn_left_open(data)` factors the pure cut-off predicate out of
`session_unfinished` — the last checkpoint lacks `ended`, in a session that
carries the field at all — and `Agent.resume` reports it as
`state_loaded {turn_open}` so a restored pane knows it may offer Continue.
Protocol §5 and the event list document the field; tests cover the predicate,
the finished/legacy false cases, and the resume event.
