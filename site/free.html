Relay Free

Open Relay, ask the agent something, and it answers. No API key, no account. Relay Free is a modest daily allowance included with Relay; when you want more, or want your prompts to go straight to a provider of your choice, add your own key and Relay Free steps out of the way.

What you get

  • The three model tiers Relay uses everywhere: Main for the agent and its planning, Flash for terminal work and quick turns, Lite for titles, labels and routing. Today they are GLM-5.3-Flash, DeepSeek V4.1 Flash and Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite, served through OpenRouter; the mapping lives on the server and can change without a new Relay.
  • A daily token allowance that resets every day. The pane shows how much is left, and when it is used up the terminal keeps working; only the agent waits for the reset or for a key of your own.
  • No sign-up. Relay makes a keypair on first use, keeps the private half in your desktop keyring, and proves it holds the key to get a token that lasts an hour.

What leaves your machine

  • On Relay Free your agent prompts, the conversation and the results of the agent's tools (command output, file contents it reads) go to Relay's gateway at api.relay-terminal.ai and on to the provider serving that tier. Their privacy policy applies to what they receive.
  • The gateway logs one line of metadata per request: time, a hash of the installation id, the tier, the provider, the status, timings and token counts. Never the text, never file contents, never terminal output. Metadata is kept for seven days.
  • Relay itself still has no telemetry: no analytics, no crash reporting. Shell commands you run, and terminal output, are never sent on their own.

Your key, whenever you want it

Options › Models › API keys… takes a key for GLM, Kimi, MiniMax, OpenRouter, OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint. Once one is stored, new panes use it, requests go directly to that provider, and nothing passes through Relay's gateway. Relay Free stays in the model list as one more choice, or you can remove it.

Limits, plainly

The allowance is a resource quota, not a crippled product: the same models, the same streaming, the same tools. Limits are enforced on the server, so a modified Relay cannot raise them, and a global spending cap means Relay Free can be "temporarily unavailable" rather than surprising anyone with a bill. Heavy use is what your own key is for.
